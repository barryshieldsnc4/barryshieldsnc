Card for a book/writing project: cover art, status pill (Published / In Progress / Draft), title, subtitle, blurb, and an optional link. Lay several out in a grid on a "Books" or "Writing" page.

```jsx
<BookCard status="Published" title="Corporate IQ for Managers"
  subtitle="Barry Shields" description="A field guide to leading teams through the AI transition."
  cover={<img src="cover.jpg" />} />
```
