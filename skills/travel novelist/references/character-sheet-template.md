# Character Sheet Template

One sheet per named character who appears in the manuscript, or who is likely to. Save each as `characters/<name>.md` in the packet. The bible's **Cast** section stays a short index (name, role, current state) and links to the sheet. The sheet holds the detail that keeps a character consistent from scene to scene.

```markdown
---
name: <character name>
role: protagonist | antagonist | ally | foil | minor
first-appears: <scene number, or "planned">
status: active | offstage | resolved
---

# <Name>

## Want and wound
- **Want:** what they are actively pursuing, in one sentence.
- **Wound:** what happened to them that shapes how they pursue it.
- **Contradiction:** one thing about them that doesn't fit the rest.

## Built from (source)
Which logged memories and real people this character draws on, listed as
composites. Record only gestures, lines, garments and habits, never a
whole real person. Example: "Gesture from M-004 (ferryman); line from
M-011 (hotel clerk)."

## Surface
- Appearance and how they carry themselves: 2-3 concrete details.
- Voice: how they talk (sentence length, what they avoid saying, any verbal tic).
- Habits and objects: what they do with their hands, what they carry.

## Relationships
One line per other character: what each wants from the other, and what
is unsaid between them.

## Arc
- **Start:** where they are when we meet them.
- **Pressure:** what the journey does to them.
- **Current:** where they are as of the latest scene.
- **Direction:** where the story seems to be taking them (may change).

## Appearances
Scene-by-scene log: scene file, what they did, what changed for them.

## Continuity facts
Anything established in the manuscript that must stay true (age, name
spelling, a scar, what they know and when they learned it).
```

## Rules

- **Composites by default.** A sheet's "Built from" section is the record that protects real people and keeps the manuscript fiction. Keep it accurate.
- **Update on every Weave.** Add the scene to Appearances, update Current and Continuity facts, and add any newly invented detail.
- **Manuscript wins.** If a sheet contradicts a written scene, fix the sheet.
- **Keep it short.** A minor character may only need Want, Surface and Continuity facts. Fill the rest as they grow.
- **Voice matters most.** When a character speaks, check their Voice line first. Two characters who sound alike are a continuity failure.
