# Story Bible Template

Create `story-bible.md` in the packet root with this structure. Keep every section current — the bible is what lets a fresh session pick up the novel cold.

```markdown
---
title: <working title>
started: <date>
trip: <where / rough dates>
distance-from-life: thin veil | transposed | fuel only
pov: <e.g., first person past>
status: <scene count> scenes, ~<n> words
---

# <Working Title> — Story Bible

## Premise
One sentence: protagonist + want + obstacle. Below it, a short paragraph
on what the book is *about* (the thematic question).

## Tone anchors
2–3 comps or adjectives, plus 1–2 lines on prose register
(e.g., "wry, close-third warmth; sentences that breathe").

## Protagonist
Name, want, wound, how they differ from the author. What pressure
the journey puts on them.

## Cast
One entry per named character:
- **Name** — role in story; built from (which logged memories/people,
  as composites); current state in the plot.

## Threads
The open promises of the book. One line each, with status:
- <thread> — OPEN | ADVANCING | RESOLVED (scene ref)

## Structure so far
Ordered scene list: file name, one-line summary, which commandment-turn
it lands (what changed).

## Rules of this world
Continuity facts the manuscript has established (place names as
fictionalized, timeline, invented details that must stay consistent).
```

Update discipline:
- Every Weave updates **Cast**, **Threads**, **Structure so far**, and the `status` line.
- Setup fills Premise, Tone, Protagonist, and frontmatter; Cast and Threads may start nearly empty. That's healthy — they grow from the ledger.
- Never let the bible contradict the manuscript. On conflict, the manuscript wins; fix the bible.
