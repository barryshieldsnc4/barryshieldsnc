# Hard Decision (WRAP)

Walks one hard decision through the WRAP method (Chip and Dan Heath, as summarized in Nir Eyal's "How to Make a Hard Decision") to reach a committed choice and a plan for if it goes badly.

## When to use it
- Job offers, big hires, hard conversations, commitments
- Anything you keep replaying at 2 a.m.
- "I can't decide", "I keep going back and forth", "am I making a mistake?"

## When it won't help
- Trivial or easily reversible choices: it will say so and help you pick quickly.
- Life-scale decisions needing legal, medical or financial expertise: it runs the process but says a professional should weigh in.
- If you're acutely upset, it slows down and checks in before pushing a decision.

## The process
1. **Pre-check:** what is actually in the way.
2. **Widen your options:** go beyond yes/no.
3. **Reality-test your assumptions:** find the cheapest way to check each one.
4. **Attain distance:** what would you tell a friend, plus a 10/10/10 lens.
5. **Prepare to be wrong:** a tripwire, a response, what is reversible, and a review date.

It ends with a decision card covering the decision, why, the values it serves, what you gave up, the plan if it goes badly, and a review date.
