# Occasion Library — per-occasion raw material

For each occasion: the **honoree set**, the **shallow take** to concede-and-reverse in Beat 1, **everyday specifics** for the Beat 3 catalog, **adjacent figures** for the Beat 6 embrace, a **register default**, and any **sensitivity notes**. Pull from these; don't treat them as a script — they're the clay, the sequence playbook is the wheel.

---

## Father's Day

- **Honoree set:** fathers, grandfathers, stepfathers, mentors, father figures.
- **Shallow take to reverse:** "just another date on the calendar — cards, gifts, family photos."
- **Everyday specifics:** early mornings and late nights; worries kept to themselves; teaching a child to ride a bike; encouragement after a hard day; showing up at school events; life lessons at the dinner table; the steady presence when life feels uncertain.
- **Reframes available:** provider/protector/problem-solver (visible) → the behind-the-scenes work (invisible); grand gestures → everyday moments; perfection → commitment; strength-as-resilience → strength-as-kindness/patience.
- **Adjacent figures (embrace):** stepfathers, grandfathers, mentors, coaches, uncles, "the men who stepped in."
- **Register default:** social, warm, ~200–260 words, ❤️👔 + #FathersDay #Dad #Family.
- **Sensitivity:** grief-heavy for those who lost or never had a father. The Beat 6 embrace ("every father figure who helped shape a life") quietly includes them — keep it.

## Mother's Day

- **Honoree set:** mothers, grandmothers, stepmothers, mentors, mother figures.
- **Shallow take to reverse:** "flowers, brunch, and a card."
- **Everyday specifics:** the invisible mental load; remembering everyone's everything; comfort in the night; the sacrifice that's never announced; teaching by example; the door always open.
- **Reframes:** the nurturing image (visible) → the relentless unseen labor (invisible); the perfect-mother myth → the present, trying, loving-anyway mother.
- **Adjacent figures:** grandmothers, stepmothers, aunts, mentors, "the women who mothered us when we needed it."
- **Register default:** social, warm, 🌷 + #MothersDay.
- **Sensitivity:** acutely painful for those grieving a mother, estranged, or facing infertility/loss. Make the embrace genuinely wide; avoid assuming everyone's mother was good or living.

## Veterans Day / Memorial Day

- **Honoree set:** *Veterans Day* = those who served (living). *Memorial Day* = those who died in service. **Do not conflate them** — this is the most common and most offensive error.
- **Shallow take to reverse:** "a day off, a sale, a long weekend."
- **Everyday specifics (Veterans):** the years given up; families left behind; skills carried quietly into civilian life; the things they don't talk about; showing up for each other still.
- **Everyday specifics (Memorial):** empty chairs at tables; names on stone; the futures they traded for ours; families who carry the cost daily.
- **Reframes:** uniform/medals (visible) → the private weight carried home; "heroes" abstraction → specific ordinary courage.
- **Adjacent figures:** all branches, reservists, families, Gold Star families (Memorial Day).
- **Register default:** more solemn; lighter on emoji; #VeteransDay or #MemorialDay.
- **Sensitivity:** Memorial Day is mourning, not thanks — "thank you for your service" is wrong for the fallen. Close with remembrance, not celebration.

## Retirement

- **Honoree set:** the retiree, by name.
- **Shallow take to reverse:** "the end of a career / clearing out a desk."
- **Everyday specifics:** the people they mentored without being asked; the calm they brought to chaos; the institutional memory only they held; the standard they set that everyone quietly copied; the early arrivals and late stays no one logged.
- **Reframes:** the title/tenure (visible) → the culture they shaped (invisible); "the end" → a legacy that keeps working after they leave.
- **Adjacent figures:** everyone they trained, the team they built.
- **Register default:** toast or tribute; personal; name specific shared memories if known. End on direct address to them by name.
- **Sensitivity:** make it about their impact, not their age. Avoid "enjoy the rest" clichés.

## Work anniversary / Team milestone

- **Honoree set:** an individual's tenure, or a team/company milestone.
- **Shallow take to reverse:** "a number on a calendar / a slide in the all-hands."
- **Everyday specifics:** the unglamorous reliability; showing up on the hard days; the quiet wins that compounded; the trust built one delivery at a time.
- **Reframes:** the metric (visible) → the people and habits behind it (invisible).
- **Adjacent figures:** the whole team, the ones who built it together.
- **Register default:** social or internal-comms; warm but professional; sparing emoji.
- **Sensitivity:** keep it genuine, not corporate-cheery; specifics prevent it from sounding like an HR template.

## Memorial / Tribute / Eulogy

- **Honoree set:** the person who died, by name; the family.
- **Shallow take to reverse:** use gently — "we gather to mourn a loss" → "but we're really here to celebrate a life."
- **Everyday specifics:** the small habits people will miss; the way they made others feel; the lessons left behind; a specific story if you have one.
- **Reframes:** the fact of death (visible) → the ongoing life of their influence (invisible); grief → gratitude (without erasing the grief).
- **Adjacent figures:** everyone they touched.
- **Register default:** tribute/eulogy; expanded Beats 2–5; a story between Beats 4 and 5; reverent.
- **Sensitivity:** highest care. Never minimize the loss to reach the uplift; hold both. Confirm consent before naming private details. End on direct address — to them or to the grieving.

## Graduation

- **Honoree set:** graduates; also the families and teachers behind them.
- **Shallow take to reverse:** "a cap, a gown, and a piece of paper."
- **Everyday specifics:** the late-night studying; the doubts pushed through; the people who believed first; the small failures that taught the most.
- **Reframes:** the diploma (visible) → the resilience earned (invisible); "the finish line" → a starting line.
- **Adjacent figures:** parents, mentors, teachers, "everyone who got them here."
- **Register default:** social/warm, 🎓 + #Graduation #ClassOf____.

## Seasonal greetings (New Year, Thanksgiving, holidays)

- **Honoree set:** the community/audience; sometimes a specific group (employees, customers, congregation).
- **Shallow take to reverse:** "lights, sales, and a date off work" / "resolutions we'll forget by February."
- **Everyday specifics:** tied to the season's real meaning — gathering, gratitude, renewal, presence over presents.
- **Reframes:** the commercial surface → the human core (belonging, thankfulness, hope).
- **Adjacent figures:** the whole community, especially those for whom the season is hard.
- **Register default:** warm, inclusive; season-appropriate emoji/hashtags.
- **Sensitivity:** holidays are isolating for many. Make room in the embrace beat.

## Occasion not listed — template

When the occasion isn't here, build the row yourself before drafting:

1. **Honoree set** — who exactly, plus the adjacent figures who belong in the embrace.
2. **Shallow take** — what does the cynic/busy person reduce this to? That's your Beat 1 concession.
3. **Everyday specifics** — list 5–8 concrete, true small acts or details unique to this honoree. (If you can't, ask the user — this is the load-bearing material.)
4. **The deeper truth** — the one non-obvious thing the message exists to say (the spine, set in the orchestration skill's outcome step).
5. **Register + sensitivities** — venue, voice, and who in the audience might be hurting.

Then run the seven-beat sequence as normal.
