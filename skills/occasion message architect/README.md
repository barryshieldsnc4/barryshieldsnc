# Occasion Message Architect

Writes warm, public-facing tribute and occasion messages, such as Father's Day posts, retirement tributes, anniversaries, memorials and holiday greetings. Every message runs on a repeatable "reframe-and-elevate" sequence.

## When to use it
- "Write a Father's Day post"
- "Draft a retirement tribute"
- "I need something for our team's anniversary"
- "Say a few words for..."
- You paste an example and want its tone and structure matched

It is for messages meant to be shared aloud or published, not transactional copy.

## How it works
It settles three things first: the occasion and honoree, the venue and voice, and the deeper truth the message exists to say. Then it writes a seven-beat sequence built on "some may say X... but really Y":

1. The Dismissive Open
2. The Hidden Layer
3. The Everyday Catalog
4. The Timeless Bridge
5. The Permission / Redefinition
6. The Widening Embrace
7. The Direct Address + Close

The finished message is tuned for its venue (social post, card, toast, tribute) and delivered ready to publish or speak.

## Files
- `SKILL.md`: the full skill
- `references/sequence-playbook.md`: move-by-move construction, phrase bank, rhythm rules and failure modes
- `references/occasion-library.md`: per-occasion material, including sensitivity notes
