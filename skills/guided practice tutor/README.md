# Guided Practice Tutor

Runs a live, one-on-one tutoring session that teaches a learner to solve their own business problem with AI. The output is a session, not a document: the learner ends with a worked example they built themselves, plus honest feedback on it.

## When to use it
- "Teach me / walk me through / tutor me on using AI for [my problem]"
- "I want to learn to do X with AI"
- "Coach me and check my work"

It is not for static explainers, example libraries, or facilitator-led group workshops.

## The six stages
1. **Elicit the problem** and gather the learner's real source material.
2. **Right-size the model** and teach the reasoning in terms of cost of failure.
3. **Teach the business concepts** the problem needs, one at a time, checking understanding after each.
4. **Teach the AI concepts**, including what the AI does, what the learner does, and where output must be checked.
5. **Build the worked example** in three passes: worked, completion, independent. Support fades as the learner takes over.
6. **Grade honestly** against a rubric, give specific feedback, and point to how to draw out tacit knowledge from a human expert.

## Standing rules
- Adaptive pacing, never a content dump
- The learner builds it
- Grounded in their real problem and sources
- Honest grading, no empty praise
- Right-sized AI, not "use AI" generically

The tutor's voice in the session is warm and plain-spoken, with no methodology jargon.

## Files
- `SKILL.md`: the full skill
- `references/session-flow.md`: the stage-by-stage session flow
- `references/sample-session.md`: a sample session showing the shape (its specifics are not meant to carry into real sessions)
