# Capture entry format

Each entry in capture-log.md follows this shape:

```
## C-NNN · [date reported] · [source]

**Raw:** [what was actually said, close to verbatim — don't clean it up
or interpret yet]

**Tag:** NEW FACT | CONTRADICTS [C-ref or state.md section] | DECISION |
QUESTION | COLOR | LENS

**Status:** UNRESOLVED | FOLDED INTO STATE | SUPERSEDED BY [C-ref]
```

## Numbering

Sequential, never reused: C-001, C-002, etc. Continues across sessions —
always check the last entry number before adding a new one.

## Tag definitions

- **NEW FACT** — a piece of information not previously captured (a test
  result, a symptom, a schedule change). Doesn't necessarily update
  state.md by itself — wait for a brief, unless it's urgent (see below).
- **CONTRADICTS** — disagrees with something already captured or already
  in state.md. Always reference what it contradicts. Always also gets
  flagged in state.md's Open Conflicts section immediately, not just
  logged and left for the next brief.
- **DECISION** — someone with the standing to decide (the patient, a
  family member with authority, a doctor) made an actual call. This is
  the only tag that goes straight into state.md's Decisions Log on the
  next brief, dated and attributed.
- **QUESTION** — something nobody has answered yet. Goes into state.md's
  Open Questions on the next brief.
- **LENS** — questions or decoded terminology generated by a clinical
  lens (see `references/lenses/`). Not a report from anyone. Always
  recorded with the lens that produced it and the date, never with a human
  source's name. A LENS entry can only ever reach state.md's Open
  Questions, marked `(lens — not from a clinician)`. It cannot become a
  fact, cannot resolve an Open Conflict, and cannot be cited later as
  something someone said.
- **COLOR** — context that doesn't change the picture (how someone's
  mood was, a kind thing the nurse said) but might matter later or is
  worth remembering. Logged, not necessarily surfaced in the state
  summary.

## When to flag urgently vs. wait for the next brief

Most captures just get logged and wait for the next brief to be folded into
state.md. Two exceptions act immediately, in the same turn as the capture:

1. **A new conflict** — goes into state.md's Open Conflicts right away.
2. **Something that reads as urgent or safety-relevant** (a medication
   error, a sudden symptom change, anything the user's tone suggests is
   time-sensitive) — say so plainly and suggest they confirm with the
   care team directly; don't just quietly log it and move on.

## Source attribution

Be specific and consistent about source naming across entries — "Dad" not
sometimes "Dad" and sometimes "my father." If the same source is described
differently in different entries, that fragmentation is exactly what this
skill exists to prevent. Ask the user once, early, how they want each
recurring source labeled, and reuse it.
